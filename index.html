The Roots Remember Fire

103,000 words · Adult Literary Fantasy

Book One

Complete manuscript seeking representation

Lysandra has spent her life in Lulath, a remote Arborian village where the elders preach caution and the past is carefully guarded. But when she pushes her magic too far, she glimpses a memory of fire—and learns her people are survivors of a genocide the world believes succeeded.

Guided by Tim, who remembers the fall, Lysandra journeys beyond the mountains into cities, politics, and dangers she never imagined. The hunters who destroyed her civilization generations ago are still searching. And some secrets, once unearthed, demand a reckoning.

Ecological memory · Cultural survival · Moral complexity · The cost of visibility
